## Transcription

### Infantry fighting rules

At the event we will apply a competitive fighting system, inspired by historical techniques, armour and weapons.

All weapons used on the battlefield must be blunt with at least 2mm edge and a tip rounded like a thumb/a Euro coin. No sharp weapons are allowed on the battlefield! The rule is always: One hit, one kill! This means when you get hit one time on a target zone, you are dead. Minimum armour requirements for fighting is: helmet, gambeson and gauntlets.

You cannot get killed by receiving a hit on plate, unless it is a vertical strike on an open faced helmet (see drawings for examples). If you receive a vertical strike on an open face helmet, you die.

You cannot get killed by strikes to the head if you wear a helmet with face protection, such as bascinet/great bascinet with visor or great helm.

The rest of the body has target zones where there isn’t any plate (see drawings for examples). You die if you receive a strike or a stab to these target zones.

All strikes or stabs on target zones must be done controlled and with restraint to reduce the risk of damages. Control and restraint must also be kept against vulnerable areas, such as joints that aren’t protected by armour. You are not allowed to hit the neck or spine of your opponent.

### Game rules

Each of the two teams of fighting infantry has their own “resurrection zone” which is a selected area, next to the battlefield. Once you die you go to your team’s resurrection zone. When the resurrection zone is filled with e.g. 5 fighters, the fighter who was first killed, re-enters the match. Afterwards the second who got killed can re-enter, etc.

After a selected time period there will be a loud sound (a horn or a gong) signalling that the next time you die, you cannot resurrect. Once a team of fighters have killed all their remaining opponents this team is declared the winners!

Depending on how enthusiastic the fighters are, a new time period can be chosen and a new battle initiated.
